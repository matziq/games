Blood Joust

Only the highest shall survive.

Fly above your enemies to win each joust.

Keyboard: A / D or arrow keys to move; Space / W to flap; hold S / ↓ to dive.

Hold ◀ ▶ to move. Tap FLAP or the arena to fly.

How to play

  • Controls: move with A / D or the arrow keys. Press Space / W to flap. Hold S / ↓ to dive at about twice normal falling speed.
  • Controls: hold ◀ ▶ to move. Tap or hold FLAP to fly; tapping the arena also flaps.
  • Joust: collide with an enemy from above to win. Equal-height jousters bounce apart.
  • Kill chains: defeat another enemy within 3 seconds of your last kill for 2x points, then 3x, 4x, and so on. Each kill refreshes the window. Pausing freezes it; losing a life ends the chain.
  • Eggs: collect dropped eggs for 250 points before they hatch. Clear all enemies and eggs to advance.
  • Egg rush: every fourth wave is a field of fast-hatching eggs. Grab them all before any hatch for a 1,000-point bonus.
  • Pterodactyl: from wave 3 it swoops in now and then. Only your lance in its open, roaring mouth (watch for the glowing eyes and gold outline) kills it, for 1,000 points. Any other touch is fatal.
  • Rider tiers: 1 green Blood Knight, 2 cyan Storm Rider, 3 purple Shadow Rider, 4 orange Ember Lord, 5 gray/black Death Lord. Each is faster, chases more, aims better, and rests less. Eggs hatch one tier higher, capped at 5.
  • Platforms are solid on every side; arenas change every three waves. A walkable bridge covers the lava through wave 4 and burns away for wave 5.
  • Skeleton hands: a flashing warning marks a reach from exposed lava. Dodge it or, if grabbed, press Space/W or tap FLAP 6 times within 1.25 seconds to escape. Holding FLAP does not count. Hands cannot be killed; grabbed mobs are pulled into lava without points. You have three lives.
  • Pause with the pause button or Escape. Sound settings are on the main menu.

Sound settings

Keyboard: Left / Right adjust volume; F toggles effects; M toggles music; Escape returns.

Paused

Take a breath. Your controls reset when you resume.
