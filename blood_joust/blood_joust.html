Blood Joust

Only the highest shall survive.

Fly above your enemies to win each joust.

Keyboard: A / D or arrow keys to move; Space / W to flap.

How to play

  • Move with A / D or the arrow keys. Press Space / W to flap. On a touch screen, hold the arrows to move and tap or hold FLAP to fly; tapping the arena also flaps.
  • Collide with an enemy from above to win. Equal-height jousters bounce apart.
  • Collect dropped eggs for 250 points before they hatch. Clear all enemies and eggs to advance.
  • Platforms are solid on every side. Enemies must rest for one second after five flaps.
  • Avoid lava and escape the fiery hands by flapping. You have three lives.
  • Use Pause or Escape to pause. Sound settings are available from the main menu.

Sound settings

Keyboard: Left / Right adjust volume; F toggles effects; M toggles music; Escape returns.

Paused

Release any held controls, then press them again after resuming.
