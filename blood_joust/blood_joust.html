Blood Joust

Only the highest shall survive.

Fly above your enemies to win each joust.

Keyboard: A / D or arrow keys to move; Space / W to flap.

Hold ◀ ▶ to move. Tap FLAP or the arena to fly.

How to play

  • Controls: move with A / D or the arrow keys. Press Space / W to flap.
  • Controls: hold ◀ ▶ to move. Tap or hold FLAP to fly; tapping the arena also flaps.
  • Joust: collide with an enemy from above to win. Equal-height jousters bounce apart.
  • Kill chains: defeat another enemy within 3 seconds of your last kill for 2x points, then 3x, 4x, and so on. Each kill refreshes the window. Pausing freezes it; losing a life ends the chain.
  • Eggs: collect dropped eggs for 250 points before they hatch. Clear all enemies and eggs to advance.
  • Platforms are solid on every side. Enemies must rest for one second after five flaps.
  • Hazards: avoid lava and escape the fiery hands by flapping. You have three lives.
  • Pause with the pause button or Escape. Sound settings are on the main menu.

Sound settings

Keyboard: Left / Right adjust volume; F toggles effects; M toggles music; Escape returns.

Take a breath. Your controls reset when you resume.
