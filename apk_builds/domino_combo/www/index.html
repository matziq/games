How To Play

Drag dominos onto the board.

Tap a domino to rotate it.

Match 3+ same tiles to merge them into the next value.

Chain reactions earn bonus points!

Red 6s vanish when matched.

Chaos: matching 4+ tiles collapses the board downward. Matching 4+ sixes instead destroys and scores every tile in the ring around them — nothing falls after that blast.

Ultimate Chaos: same 4+ thresholds and six explosions, but every collapse throws the board up, down, left or right at random — and exploding sixes trigger a collapse too.

Game ends when no moves remain.
