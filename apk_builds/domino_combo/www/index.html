How To Play

Drag dominos onto the board.

Tap a domino to rotate it.

Match 3+ same tiles to merge them into the next value.

Chain reactions earn bonus points!

Match 4+ tiles to collapse the board in a random direction.

During a collapse, matching 3+ tiles collapses the board again!

Match 5+ tiles to earn a Remix Token. Use it to scramble all board pieces.

Red 6s: matching 4+ sixes charges and explodes neighboring tiles.

Game ends when no moves remain.
