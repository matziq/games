PASS & PLAY • TWO PLAYERS

Tower Tumble

Take turns dropping different shapes. Build as high as you can without letting a block fall off the platform.

Tap the board or use the slider to aim; use Left/Right arrow keys to move, R to rotate, and Space to drop when not using another control. Blocks continue moving under gravity. The player whose drop causes a fall loses; survive 20 rounds together to win.

Progress is stored on this browser and device when storage is available. If you leave while blocks are moving, the tower resumes from its last saved physics snapshot.
