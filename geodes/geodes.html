GEODES

The volcano is in the distance. Shoot geodes before they reach the laser. One geode hitting the gun ends the run.

Tap to shoot · some stay small and climb · others grow as they come at you · 10 geodes grant a missile

How to play
🌋 Geodes — Dark spheres with a crystal crack. Tap to crack them and release gems.
🪨 Rocks — Jagged gray stones. Tap to break them (no gems, no penalty for missing).
🔥 Fireballs — Rare glowing orbs. Tap to explode and turn all geodes into diamonds!
🚀 Missiles — Crack 10 geodes and a pod slides out of the gun. Right-click, long-press, or tap Arm, then tap the target. The pod retracts when you are empty.
