FIELD GUIDE

Search the entrance chest for a pistol and a survey. Recover the idol, then leave through the left edge of the entrance room's middle tier. The map shows visited rooms and the survey's clue. Nothing is awarded for unnecessary killing.

Classic commands

W / R / S
Walk / run / stop. Movement continues until stopped.
A / D
Face left / right.
J / G / C
Jump / crawl / climb a nearby stair or shaft.
O / L / T
Open / look / take. Rubble needs two opens.
F
Enter fight mode and stop.
In fight
A/D step; S turn; M slash; L lunge; G draw pistol; Space fire. W/R/J leave fight.
P
Place dynamite. A 2.4-second fuse; only one lit stick.

Read the room

Diagonal green steps connect the three tiers. Stand at an endpoint and climb. Orange UP/DN shafts link dungeon depths. Side doors connect rooms on the middle tier. Jump the platform gaps; you can also drop to a lower tier. Kneeling avoids high darts and bats. Serpents charge, spiders patrol, and guards throw darts.

Orange striped stone and cracked platform sections break under dynamite. Keep clear of your own blast! Main stairs and the bottom floor cannot be destroyed in this adaptation. Spikes and moving ceilings are dangerous. Tonics restore two strength, up to five. Health starts at three. No save game; retry regenerates the same tomb.

Touch controls always use held movement, regardless of keyboard style. Audio starts off. Reduced-motion mode removes decorative flicker and blast flashes. Leaving the tab pauses play. This spatial canvas game is not fully accessible to screen readers.
