# Bundled recording provenance

The short environmental recordings in this folder are conversions of five WAV files from Luka Aleksic's **Various Sound Effects** collection:

- `creak-floor-1.ogg` from `floor_creak1.wav`
- `creak-floor-2.ogg` from `floor_creak2.wav`
- `creak-floor-3.ogg` from `floor_creak3.wav`
- `rumble-stone.ogg` from `grind_stone.wav`
- `water-drips.ogg` from `tap_water.wav`

The creator states that the 27 recordings were recorded on a phone, cleaned in Audacity, and released under CC0. The OpenGameArt record marks the collection **CC0 1.0 Universal**:

- Collection and license: https://opengameart.org/content/various-sound-effects
- Creator: Luka Aleksic (`laleksic`)
- Original download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/sounds_8.zip
- License deed: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The selected recordings were converted to mono OGG Vorbis for compact offline delivery. No remix or additional recording was added. Attribution is not required by CC0; this provenance is included for clarity.
